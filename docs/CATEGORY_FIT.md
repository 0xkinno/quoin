# Track Strategy & Category Fit: Professional Agents

QUOIN is built specifically for the **Professional Agents** track of the AWS Agents for Humans Hackathon:

## 1. Professional Operational Workflows
Professional service businesses (agencies, consultancies, enterprise B2B support) process dozens of high-value client requests daily:
- Invoice discount requests
- Remediation credits for missed delivery SLAs
- Out-of-scope work approvals
- Contract terms adjustments

## 2. Why Professional Agents Need Policy Fences
In high-stakes professional operations, commercial policies change frequently to protect margins and cash flow.
When leadership tightens a policy (e.g., cutting maximum discount ceilings due to quarterly budget caps), in-flight automated agents operating on obsolete memory cause direct financial loss.

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop as Escalation, Not Bottleneck
Rather than forcing human review on every routine request, QUOIN allows autonomous execution when actions are provably compliant with current verified policy, and deterministically halts and escalates only when a policy race or threshold boundary is crossed.
